these components, including local modifications.

Setup, on the player's computer, downloads and uses: Forza Horizon 1:
ReImagined (Dyotson/RecompiledForzaHorizon, MIT; code-generation configuration,
game hooks and SDK patches), the ReXGlue SDK, llvm-mingw (LLVM) and the PS5
payload SDK headers.

The dynamic-buffer content tracking in the PS5 runtime patch follows the
approach of PS5X360 (BrinooTk, GPL-3.0-or-later components; reimplemented here
inside the BSD-licensed ReXGlue shared memory code, off by default).
